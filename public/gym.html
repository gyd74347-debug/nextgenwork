About Shakti Fitness

WHERE INDIA TRAINS HARD

Founded in 2012 in the heart of Delhi, Shakti Fitness is not just a gym — it's a movement. We blend ancient Indian fitness wisdom like yoga, kushti, and pranayama with modern sports science to deliver transformations that go beyond the body.

Our certified trainers are not just coaches — they are mentors, motivators, and warriors who walk the same path with you. From working professionals to national-level athletes, Shakti Fitness has shaped thousands of success stories across India.

FAQ

COMMON QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know before you join.

Is there a free trial available?+
Yes! We offer a 7-day free trial for all new members. No credit card required. Just bring a valid ID and workout clothes. You can experience all our facilities and one group class of your choice.
Do you have separate sections for women?+
Absolutely. We have a dedicated women's workout zone with separate locker rooms, female trainers available at all times, and women-only classes for yoga, Zumba, and HIIT. Your comfort and safety are our top priority.
What are your gym timings?+
Our gym is open 7 days a week. Monday to Friday: 5:00 AM – 11:00 PM. Saturday: 6:00 AM – 10:00 PM. Sunday: 7:00 AM – 8:00 PM. Elite members get 24/7 key card access.
Do you provide vegetarian-friendly nutrition plans?+
Yes, 100%. Over 60% of our members are vegetarian. Our dietitian Sunita Reddy specializes in high-performance Indian vegetarian diets using paneer, dal, sprouts, soya, and traditional superfoods like ashwagandha and moringa.
Can beginners join without any prior gym experience?+
Beginners are our specialty! Every new member gets a free orientation session, a body composition assessment, and a customized starter program. Our trainers will guide you from day one, ensuring safety and maximum results.
Do you offer corporate membership packages?+
Yes, we have special corporate wellness packages for teams of 10+. Benefits include group discounts, priority booking, on-site fitness assessment camps, and dedicated account management. Contact us for a custom quote.
Is parking available?+
We have a dedicated parking area with 80 spots for two-wheelers and 30 spots for four-wheelers. Parking is free for all members. We are also well-connected by metro — 5-minute walk from the nearest station.
